# Staff Engineer Levels at Large Companies

Staff engineer is the first level above senior engineer on the individual contributor track. Large companies use different codes and titles for it. The table below lists the titles that candidates and employees commonly report. None of these companies publishes an official ladder, and the mapping between companies is approximate.

## Commonly reported levels

| Company | Senior level | Staff level | Next level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google | L5, Senior Software Engineer | L6, Staff Software Engineer | L7, Senior Staff Software Engineer |
| Meta | E5, Senior Software Engineer | E6, Staff Software Engineer | E7 (title reported differently by different sources) |
| Amazon | L6, SDE III (often called Senior SDE) | L7, Principal SDE | L8, Senior Principal SDE |

Three cautions apply to this table.

- **The codes do not align.** Amazon has no level named "staff". L7 Principal is the level above senior, and sources disagree on whether it corresponds to Google L6 or L7.
- **Titles vary inside a company.** Teams and countries sometimes use different names for the same level code.
- **Companies change their ladders.** Confirm the current level and title with your recruiter.

This page states no pay figures. Compensation changes often and depends on location.

## What changes in expected scope

| Level | Commonly expected scope | Typical time horizon of decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Senior | One team or one service; leads projects inside the team | Months |
| Staff | Several teams or one technical area; defines problems that others solve | One to three years |
| Senior staff and principal | An organization or a company-wide technical area | Three years or more |

The interview follows the scope. A staff loop looks for at least one project that involved several teams, and a senior staff loop looks for a pattern of them.

## Smaller companies

At a company of 200 engineers, "staff engineer" may be the highest technical level, and the scope may be closer to a senior staff role at a large company. At a company of 30 engineers, the title is sometimes given for tenure. Hiring committees at large companies know this and grade the evidence, not the title on your resume.

To translate your level for a recruiter, describe scope in three numbers:

1. The number of teams your work affected.
2. The duration of your largest project.
3. One measured result.

"Staff engineer at a 40-person startup" gives a recruiter little to work with. "Led a data platform rebuild used by 9 teams over 18 months, which cut pipeline failures from 30 a week to 2" can be placed on a ladder.

## Questions to ask the recruiter

- Which level is this loop calibrated for?
- Can the committee offer a level above or below that target?
- Which rounds carry the most weight for the level decision?
- Is there a project deep dive, and how long is it?

Ask these in the first call. The answers decide how you prepare, and they show the recruiter that you understand how leveling works.

## Level and interview format

| Level interviewed for | Usual additions to the loop |
|---|---|
| Senior | Two coding rounds, one design round, one behavioral round |
| Staff | A project deep dive, a second design or behavioral round, one coding round |
| Senior staff and principal | Longer deep dives, sometimes a presentation to a panel |

These formats are commonly reported and differ by company and team. See [the interview loop](../rounds/interview-loop.md) for the full process.
